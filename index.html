Checkpoint: who decides what, and when?

Two hospitals had merged and wanted AI to predict 30-day readmissions, but nobody owned the decisions once AI was in the loop. We mapped stakeholders and the AS-IS and TO-BE workflows, then designed a decision-rights model: the AI scores and sorts patients, and four mandatory human checkpoints keep a named person accountable for identity matches, low-confidence cases, every risk tier and monthly governance.
