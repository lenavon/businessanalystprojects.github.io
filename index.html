Ghana Domestic Debt Exchange

The government swapped its bonds for new ones. I led my bank's finance work on what that cost.

In 2022–23, Ghana restructured its domestic debt and banks exchanged their government bonds for new bonds with lower coupons and longer maturities. Every bank had to measure the loss, book it correctly and explain it to the regulator. I led the finance side of that work for my bank, including computing the impairment on the exchanged bonds.

How I work

Four moves, every project.

The same sequence runs through each case above. It's how a vague request becomes something a team can build and a sponsor can fund.

01

Elicit

Interviews, workshops and the conversations people don't schedule. I listen for the problem behind the request.

→ Stakeholder map · RACI

02

Model

Draw how work really flows today, then how it should. Gaps show up in the swimlanes before they show up in production.

→ AS-IS / TO-BE · use cases

03

Decide

Weigh the options on cost, risk and fit. Recommend one, and say plainly why the others lost.

→ Options analysis · business case

04

Prove

Every requirement traces to a need, and every benefit has a number, a baseline and a review date.

→ BRD · RTM · SMART KPIs
